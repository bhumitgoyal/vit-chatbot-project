# Module 08: Academic Rules, Examination System, SAP & Global Programs

## 1. Internal & Final Examination Structure

VIT follows a rigorous continuous internal assessment pattern combined with a comprehensive Final Assessment Test (FAT).

### Theory Course Assessment Weightage Breakdown:

| Assessment Component | Duration / Format | Max Marks | Final Weightage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1)** | 90 Minutes (Descriptive / Analytical) | 50 Marks | **15%** |
| **CAT-2 (Continuous Assessment Test 2)** | 90 Minutes (Descriptive / Open Book / CBT) | 50 Marks | **15%** |
| **Digital Assignments / Quizzes / Projects** | Ongoing Semester Submission | 30 Marks | **30%** |
| **FAT (Final Assessment Test)** | 3 Hours (Comprehensive End-Sem Exam) | 100 Marks | **40%** |
| **TOTAL** | | | **100%** |

## 2. Passing Criteria & Re-Examination Rules

### Passing Conditions:
To successfully pass a theory course and earn course credits, a student must meet **BOTH** of the following requirements:
1. **FAT Minimum Requirement**: Score a minimum of **40% (40 out of 100 marks)** in the Final Assessment Test (FAT).
2. **Overall Aggregate Requirement**: Score an overall minimum aggregate of **50% (50 out of 100 final weighted marks)** across CAT-1, CAT-2, Internals, and FAT combined.

*Failure to satisfy either condition results in an 'F' (Fail) grade, requiring the student to re-register for the course.*

### Re-test Policy:
- If a student misses CAT-1 or CAT-2 due to valid medical reasons or official On-Duty (OD) sports/fest representation, they can apply for a **Re-test** subject to Proctor, HoD, and Dean approvals.

## 3. Absolute vs Relative Grading Methodology

VIT applies two grading frameworks depending on class size and nature:

### A. Relative Grading (Standard for Large Theory Classes)
- Applied when class enrollment is $> 10$ students.
- Marks distribution follows a normal Bell Curve based on **Class Mean ($\mu$)** and **Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)**.
- **S Grade (10 Points)**: Typically awarded to the top ~10% of class performance (often thresholded at $\mu + 1.5\sigma$).
- **Letter Grade Scale**:
  - **S Grade**: 10 Points (Outstanding)
  - **A Grade**: 9 Points (Excellent)
  - **B Grade**: 8 Points (Very Good)
  - **C Grade**: 7 Points (Good)
  - **D Grade**: 6 Points (Satisfactory)
  - **E Grade**: 5 Points (Pass)
  - **F Grade**: 0 Points (Fail - Re-registration required)
  - **N Grade**: 0 Points (Debarred due to Attendance `< 75%`)

### B. Absolute Grading (Lab Courses & Small Classes)
- Applied for laboratory courses, projects, or classes with $\le 10$ students.
- **S Grade**: $\ge 90\%$ overall marks.
- **A Grade**: $80\% - 89\%$.
- **B Grade**: $70\% - 79\%$.
- **C Grade**: $60\% - 69\%$.
- **D Grade**: $55\% - 59\%$.
- **E Grade**: $50\% - 54\%$.
- **F Grade**: $< 50\%$.

## 4. Academic Privileges for 9.0+ CGPA Students ("9 Pointer Club")

Students who maintain a **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) $\ge 9.00$** enjoy exclusive academic privileges:

1. **Attendance Exemption (No 75% Cap)**:
   - 9.0+ CGPA students are exempt from the mandatory 75% attendance rule. They are not debarred ('N' grade) even if attendance falls below 75%.
2. **Priority FFCS Course Bidding**:
   - Get early access slots during VTOP FFCS registration to choose top faculty members and ideal class time slots before general registration opens.
3. **Credit Overloading**:
   - Permitted to register for up to 27–30 credits per semester to fast-track their degree.

---

## 5. Backlog Clearing & Summer Term

- **Summer / Winter Vacation Term**:
  - Students with 'F' (Fail) or 'N' (Debarred) grades can register for intensive backlog clearing courses during summer or winter breaks.
- **Grade Improvement**:
  - Students receiving 'E' or 'D' grades can choose to re-register the course to improve their CGPA.

---

## 6. International Opportunities: SAP & ITP (2+2 / 3+1)

### A. Semester Abroad Program (SAP)
- Students spend their 7th or 8th semester pursuing project work or coursework at renowned partner universities across USA, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
- Credit transfer is seamless back to VIT upon submission of transcripts.

### B. International Transfer Program (ITP)
- **2+2 Program**: Students complete 2 years at VIT and the final 2 years at a partner international university, receiving a degree from the foreign university.
- **3+1 Program**: 3 years at VIT + 1 year abroad.
- **Partner Foreign Universities**:
  - **USA**: Purdue University, Binghamton University, Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), University of Massachusetts Dartmouth.
  - **Australia**: Deakin University, RMIT University, Queensland University of Technology.
  - **UK**: Newcastle University, University of Strathclyde.
