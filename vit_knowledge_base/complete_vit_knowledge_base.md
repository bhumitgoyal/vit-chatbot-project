# SECTION 1: INSTITUTION OVERVIEW, RANKINGS & CAMPUSES

## 1.1 Identity & History
- **Full Name**: Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)
- **Establishment**: Founded in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College (VEC) by Dr. G. Viswanathan. Granted Deemed University status in 2001 by MHRD, Govt. of India.
- **Status**: Recognized as an *Institution of Eminence (IoE)* by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.
- **Leadership**: Dr. G. Viswanathan (Founder & Chancellor); Mr. Sankar Viswanathan, Dr. Sekar Viswanathan, Mr. G.V. Selvam (Vice Presidents).

## 1.2 Rankings & Accreditations
- **NAAC Accreditation**: **A++ Grade** (CGPA 3.66 on a 4-point scale).
- **NIRF Rankings (2025)**:
  - University Category: **14th**
  - Research Category: **14th**
  - Engineering Category: **16th**
  - Overall Category: **21st**
- **ABET (USA)**: International ABET accreditation for B.Tech CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, and allied departments.
- **QS World University Rankings**: Ranked in 600–800 band globally; top 150 in Asia.

## 1.3 Campuses
1. **VIT Vellore (Main)**: Katpadi, Vellore, Tamil Nadu (372+ acres, 16 Schools, 35,000+ students).
2. **VIT Chennai**: Vandalur-Kelambakkam Road, Chennai, TN (170+ acres, near OMR IT corridor).
3. **VIT-AP**: Inavolu, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh (200+ acres, focus on AI, Data Science & Law).
4. **VIT Bhopal**: Sehore, Madhya Pradesh (350+ acres, Collaborative Active Learning methodology).
5. **VIT Mauritius**: Saint Pierre, Mauritius (International offshore campus).

- **Total Credits for B.Tech**: ~160 Credits over 8 semesters.

# SECTION 3: EXAMINATIONS, GRADING & ACADEMIC RULES

## 3.1 Examination Weightage
- **CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1)**: 15% Weightage (50 Marks)
- **CAT-2 (Continuous Assessment Test 2)**: 15% Weightage (50 Marks)
- **Digital Assignments / Quizzes / Internals**: 30% Weightage
- **FAT (Final Assessment Test)**: 40% Weightage (100 Marks)

## 3.2 Passing Criteria & Grading
- Must score $\ge 40\%$ in FAT (min 40/100 marks) AND $\ge 50\%$ aggregate overall.
- **Relative Grading (Large Classes)**:
  - **S Grade**: 10 Points (Outstanding - Top ~10% / Mean + $1.5\sigma$)
  - **A Grade**: 9 Points (Excellent)
  - **B Grade**: 8 Points (Very Good)
  - **C Grade**: 7 Points (Good)
  - **D Grade**: 6 Points (Satisfactory)
  - **E Grade**: 5 Points (Pass)
  - **F Grade**: 0 Points (Fail - Re-registration needed)
  - **N Grade**: 0 Points (Debarred for attendance $< 75\%$)

## 3.3 "9 Pointer Club" (CGPA $\ge 9.00$) Privileges
- Attendance exemption (no 75% cap).
- Priority slots during FFCS course registration on VTOP.
- Permission for credit overloading up to 27–30 credits/sem.

# SECTION 4: ADMISSIONS, VITEEE EXAM & RANK CUTOFFS

## 4.1 VITEEE Entrance Exam
- **Format**: Computer Based Test (CBT), 2 hours 30 minutes (150 minutes).
- **Questions**: 125 MCQs Total (Maths/Bio 40, Physics 35, Chemistry 35, Aptitude 10, English 5).
- **Marking Scheme**: **+4 Marks** for correct answer, **-1 Mark** for wrong answer.

## 4.2 Eligibility
- Minimum **60% aggregate** in Physics, Chemistry, and Maths/Biology in 12th Board Exams (50% for SC/ST and North-East candidates).
