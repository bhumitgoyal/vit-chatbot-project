# Module 04: Fee Structure, Hostel Charges & Scholarships

## 1. Annual Tuition Fee Structure (B.Tech Programs)

VIT categorizes B.Tech engineering branches into two academic fee groups (**Group A** and **Group B**) and assigns tuition fees across 5 merit-based scholarship categories (**Category 1 to Category 5**).

### Academic Branch Classification:
- **Group A Branches**: Biotechnology, Bioengineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE), Fashion Technology.
- **Group B Branches**: Computer Science & Engineering (Core & all Specializations), Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Aerospace Engineering.

## 4. Mess Types & Catering Options

Students living in VIT hostels have access to central mess halls managed by professional catering vendors.

1. **Vegetarian Mess (Veg)**:
   - 100% vegetarian Indian breakfast, lunch, evening snacks/tea, and dinner.
   - Lowest annual cost among mess options.
2. **Non-Vegetarian Mess (Non-Veg)**:
   - Includes vegetarian menu plus egg and chicken dishes served 4 to 5 times a week.
3. **Special Mess / Multi-Cuisine Mess**:
   - Offers wider menu varieties including North Indian, South Indian, Continental, Chinese, ice-creams, milkshakes, and specialized diet counters.

## 5. Scholarships & Financial Aid Framework

### A. VITEEE Merit Scholarships
- **VITEEE Rank 1 to 50**: 75% Tuition Fee Waiver across all 4 years.
- **VITEEE Rank 51 to 100**: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver across all 4 years.
- **VITEEE Rank 101 to 1000**: 25% Tuition Fee Waiver across all 4 years.

### B. STARS Scheme (Rural District Toppers)
- 100% scholarship covering Tuition, Hostel, and Mess for underprivileged rural toppers.

### C. Sports Scholarships
- 100% to 50% fee waivers for students who have represented India at international levels or won national championships.

### D. Merit-Cum-Means & Special Assistance
- Financial assistance for students belonging to low-income families maintaining CGPA > 8.0.

---

## 6. Fee Refund Policy (UGC Compliant)

VIT adheres to UGC guidelines regarding fee refunds upon admission cancellation:
- **Notice 15+ Days before formally notified last date**: 100% refund of tuition fees (less up to ₹5,000 processing fee).
- **Less than 15 Days before last date**: 90% refund.
- **Within 15 Days after last date**: 80% refund.
- **30 Days or more after last date**: 0% tuition refund (Only caution deposits refunded).
