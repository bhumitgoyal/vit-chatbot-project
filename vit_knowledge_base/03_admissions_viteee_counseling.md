# Module 03: Admissions, VITEEE Exam & Counseling Process

## 1. Overview of Admissions

Admission to undergraduate engineering (B.Tech) programs across all campuses of Vellore Institute of Technology (Vellore, Chennai, AP Amaravati, Bhopal) is conducted primarily through the **VIT Engineering Entrance Examination (VITEEE)**.

---

## 2. VITEEE Exam Pattern & Test Structure

VITEEE is a proctored Computer Based Test (CBT) conducted across 120+ test cities in India and select international centers (Dubai, Kuwait, Muscat, Qatar, Singapore).

### Exam Summary Table:

| Parameter | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Mode** | Computer Based Test (CBT) |
| **Duration** | 2 Hours 30 Minutes (150 Minutes) |
| **Medium** | English |
| **Total Questions** | 125 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) |
| **Marking Scheme** | **+4 Marks** for each correct answer; **-1 Mark** for incorrect answer |
| **Negative Marking** | Yes (-1 mark penalty for wrong choices) |

### Subject-Wise Question Breakdown:

| Subject / Section | Number of Questions | Weightage / Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mathematics / Biology** | 40 Questions | 160 Marks |
| **Physics** | 35 Questions | 140 Marks |
| **Chemistry** | 35 Questions | 140 Marks |
| **Aptitude** | 10 Questions | 40 Marks |
| **English** | 05 Questions | 20 Marks |
| **TOTAL** | **125 Questions** | **500 Marks Total Scale** |

*Note: Mathematics stream candidates attempt MPCEA (Maths, Physics, Chemistry, English, Aptitude); Biology stream candidates attempt BPCEA (Biology, Physics, Chemistry, English, Aptitude) for Biotech/Biomedical tracks.*

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria for B.Tech Admissions

1. **Nationality**:
   - Resident Indian / Non-Resident Indian (NRI) / PIO / OCI card holders / Foreign Nationals.
2. **Academic Qualification**:
   - Pass in 10+2 / Higher Secondary Examination conducted by CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, or equivalent.
3. **Minimum Marks Aggregate**:
   - **General / OBC**: Minimum aggregate of **60% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology (PCM/PCB)** in 12th board exams.
   - **Relaxed Category (SC / ST / North Eastern States / J&K / Ladakh)**: Minimum aggregate of **50% in PCM/PCB**.
4. **Age Limit**:
   - Candidates must be born on or after **July 1st of the target admission year limit** (typically under 22 years of age at the time of entry).

---

## 4. Slot Booking & OTBS System

Before taking the VITEEE exam, applicants must book their exam date, session slot, and exam center via the **Online Test Booking System (OTBS)** portal:
- Slot booking is offered on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Hall Ticket / Admit Card is generated instantly upon successful OTBS slot selection.

---

## 5. VITEEE Counseling & Seat Allocation Process

VITEEE results are published as a **Merit Rank** (No official marks scorecards released). Seat allotment is strictly merit-driven across 5 Fee Categories.

### Counseling Phases (Indicative Rank Slabs):
- **Phase 1**: Ranks 1 to 20,000
- **Phase 2**: Ranks 20,001 to 45,000
- **Phase 3**: Ranks 45,001 to 70,000
- **Phase 4**: Ranks 70,001 to 1,00,000
- **Phase 5**: Ranks > 1,00,000 (Primarily for VIT-AP and VIT Bhopal campuses)

### How Category Choice Filling Works:
During counseling, students select their preferred **Campus**, **Branch**, and **Fee Category** (Category 1 to Category 5):
- Lower rank numbers (e.g., Rank 1 to 5,000) get early access to Category 1 (lowest tuition fee) for popular branches like B.Tech CSE Vellore.
- As Category 1 seats fill up, subsequent ranks can opt for Category 2, 3, 4, or 5 for the same branch or choose Category 1 in another branch or campus.
- **Permanent Category**: Once allotted and accepted during counseling, the fee category remains fixed for all 4 years of study.

## 6. STARS Scheme & Special Scholarships

### A. STARS (Support the Advancement of Rural Students) Scheme
- **Objective**: To provide higher education to rural, underprivileged students from government schools.
- **Benefits**: **100% Free Education** including 100% tuition fee waiver, 100% hostel room fee waiver, and 100% food/mess fee waiver for 4 years.
- **Eligibility**: Top boy student and top girl student from rural government higher secondary schools in each district of Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

### B. Board Toppers Scholarship
- 100% tuition fee waiver for all 4 years for Central Board (CBSE/ICSE) and State Board **Rank 1 Toppers**.

### C. VITEEE Merit Scholarships:
- **Ranks 1 to 50**: 75% Tuition Fee Waiver for 4 years.
- **Ranks 51 to 100**: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver for 4 years.
- **Ranks 101 to 1000**: 25% Tuition Fee Waiver for 4 years.

## 7. PG Admissions (VITMEE) & Ph.D Admissions (VITREE)

- **VITMEE (VIT Master's Entrance Examination)**: CBT exam for admission into M.Tech and MCA programs. GATE scorecard holders receive direct preference and stipend opportunities.
- **VITREE (VIT Research Entrance Examination)**: CBT + Interview process for admission to Ph.D and M.Tech (Research) degrees, conducted twice a year (July and January sessions).
