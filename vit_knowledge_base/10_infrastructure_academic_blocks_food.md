# Module 10: Infrastructure, Academic Buildings, Library & Food Joints

## 1. Academic Buildings & Landmarks (VIT Vellore)

The Vellore campus covers over 372 acres of lush, eco-friendly infrastructure featuring iconic multi-story academic buildings:

### A. Technology Tower (TT)
- **Overview**: An iconic 7-story central building that serves as a core academic landmark on campus.
- **Schools Housed**: School of Electrical Engineering (SELECT), School of Electronics Engineering (SENSE), School of Advanced Sciences (SAS), School of Social Sciences & Languages (SSL).
- **Facilities**:
  - Houses **VITTBI** (Technology Business Incubator).
  - Major Auditoriums: **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Auditorium** and **Kamaraj Auditorium** (hosting keynotes, seminars, and club events).

### B. Silver Jubilee Tower (SJT)
- **Overview**: A state-of-the-art high-rise tower located near the women's hostel blocks and main sports fields.
- **Schools Housed**: School of Computer Science & Engineering (SCOPE), School of Information Technology (SITE).
- **Key Offices**:
  - **Academic Office** (Transcript issuing, FFCS help desk).
  - **Career Development Centre (CDC)** placement office.
- **Facilities**: Features 100+ smart multimedia classrooms and cutting-edge software computing laboratories equipped with high-end GPUs.

### C. Main Building (Dr. MGR Block)
- **Overview**: The historic foundational building of the university campus.
- **Key Offices**: Chancellor's Office, Vice Presidents' Offices, Vice-Chancellor's Office, Central Finance Office, Registrar Office, and Admissions Helpdesk.

### D. G.D. Naidu Block & M. Visvesvaraya Block (MVK)
- **Schools Housed**: School of Mechanical Engineering (SMEC), School of Chemical Engineering (SCHEME), School of Civil Engineering (SCE).
- **Facilities**: Heavy machine workshops, CAD/CAM labs, wind tunnel testing bays, thermal engineering labs, and automotive testing bays.

## 2. Periyar EVR Central Library

The **Periyar EVR Central Library** is a modern 7-story (Ground + 6 floors) fully automated library building spanning over 150,000 sq. ft.

### Resources & Digital Library:
- **Print Collection**: Over 240,000+ printed books, reference volumes, and technical manuals.
- **Digital Subscriptions**: Access to 300,000+ e-books and top digital research databases including IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Scopus, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, ASCE, and ASME.
- **Automation**: RFID-based automated check-in and self-checkout kiosks.
- **Exam Reading Halls**: Air-conditioned silent reading halls open **24/7** during CAT and FAT examination weeks.

## 3. On-Campus Food Courts & Social Hangout Spots

VIT Vellore offers a diverse culinary environment with multi-cuisine dining options:

- **Gazebo**: The most famous informal outdoor central square on campus, where students gather in the evenings for discussions, club meetups, and open-air performances.
- **Food Street**: Located near D-Annex and hostel zones, offering a row of fast-food counters, wraps, juices, and regional Indian snacks.
- **Commercial Dining & Cafes**:
  - **Domino's Pizza** outlet inside campus.
  - **Subway** sandwich counter.
  - **Woodys** & **Kathi Rolls**.
  - **Baskin Robbins** ice-cream parlor.
  - **Nescafe Kiosks** located outside TT, SJT, and main academic blocks for quick coffee/snacks.
  - **DC (Dining Centre) & KC Food Courts**: Multi-cuisine food courts serving North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, and Continental meals.

## 4. Transit & Travel Connectivity

VIT Vellore's strategic location provides smooth transit to major metro hubs:

- **Nearest Railway Station**: **Katpadi Junction (KPD)** – 3 km from main gate. (Direct Shatabdi, Vande Bharat, and Brindavan Express connections to Chennai and Bangalore).
- **Nearest Bus Terminal**: Vellore New Bus Stand (5 km).
- **Nearest Airports**:
  - **Chennai International Airport (MAA)**: ~130 km (2.5 hours via Chennai-Bangalore Highway NH48).
  - **Kempegowda International Airport Bengaluru (BLR)**: ~210 km (3.5 to 4 hours).
