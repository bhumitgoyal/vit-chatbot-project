## 3. Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS)

VIT pioneered the **Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS)** in India, empowering students to build their own custom academic journey.

### Key Highlights of FFCS:
1. **Freedom of Choice**:
   - Students select their courses, preferred faculty members, lecture time slots, and lab slots every semester.
2. **Timetable Customization via VTOP Portal**:
   - Registration takes place on the **VTOP** student portal before each semester begins in a competitive real-time bidding process.
3. **Pacing Your Degree**:
   - Fast-track learning: Students can register for extra credits (up to 27 credits per semester) to complete degree requirements early or free up the 8th semester for full-time internships.
   - Slow-track option: Option to take fewer credits per semester if extra focus is needed.
4. **Interdisciplinary & Audit Courses**:
   - Students can choose University Electives outside their major department (e.g., a CSE student taking Music, German Language, Financial Accounting, or Astronomy).

### Credit Distribution (B.Tech ~160 Total Credits):
- **Engineering Foundation & Sciences**: ~50 Credits
- **Program Core**: ~50 Credits
- **Program Electives**: ~24 Credits
- **University Electives (Open/Interdisciplinary)**: ~12 Credits
- **Project Work & Internship**: ~24 Credits

## 4. Grading System & CGPA Calculation

VIT uses a **Relative Grading Framework** based on standard normal distribution curves.

### Grade Scale & Grade Points:

| Grade | Letter Meaning | Grade Points | Performance Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S** | Outstanding | **10** | Top tier (~top 5-10% of class curve) |
| **A** | Excellent | **9** | Very High Performance |
| **B** | Very Good | **8** | Above Average |
| **C** | Good | **7** | Average Performance |
| **D** | Satisfactory | **6** | Below Average |
| **E** | Pass | **5** | Minimum passing grade |
| **F** | Fail | **0** | Must re-register and re-do course |
| **N** | Debarred | **0** | Attendance `< 75%`, not allowed for exams |

### Attendance Criteria:
- **75% Mandatory Attendance**: Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in theory and lab classes.
- **Debarment (N Grade)**: Falling below 75% attendance results in an 'N' grade, preventing the student from writing the final examination (FAT) and requiring them to re-register the course under FFCS.
- **90%+ Attendance Benefit**: High-attendance incentives or relaxation during specific medical emergencies subject to Proctor approval.

## 5. VTOP Student Portal

**VTOP (VIT On-line Portal)** is the central ERP digital platform used by students, parents, and faculty:
- **Key Features**:
  - FFCS course registration & timetable generation.
  - Live attendance tracker and marks viewer (CAT-1, CAT-2, Assignments, Quiz, FAT).
  - Digital library access and fee payment gateway.
  - Hostel leave request processing (Outing / Vacation leaves).
  - Proctor interaction and official transcript requests.
