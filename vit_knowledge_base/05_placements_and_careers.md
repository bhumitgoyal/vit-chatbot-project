# Module 05: Placements, Internships & Career Development Centre (CDC)

## 1. Overview of Placements at VIT

VIT has achieved national fame for its stellar placement records. All placement activities across the four Indian campuses (**Vellore, Chennai, AP Amaravati, and Bhopal**) are **centralized** through the main campus **Career Development Centre (CDC)**, giving students across all campuses equal access to top global recruiters.

## 3. Placement Categories & Slotting Framework

VIT categorizes placement campus drives based on the offered Cost to Company (CTC):

1. **Super Dream Offers (CTC ≥ ₹10 LPA)**:
   - Reserved for top tech giants, product companies, and high-frequency trading (HFT) firms.
   - Companies include: Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Adobe, Qualcomm, Intel, Nvidia, Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, D.E. Shaw, PayPal, Cisco, Atlassian.
2. **Dream Offers (CTC ₹6 LPA to ₹10 LPA)**:
   - Offered by high-tier IT product, analytics, and consulting firms.
   - Companies include: Deloitte, Accenture (FTE/Advanced), Schneider Electric, Fidelity, HP, Dell, SAP Labs, Hyundai, Bosch.
3. **Regular / Core Offers (CTC < ₹6 LPA)**:
   - IT service majors and core domain engineering companies.
   - Companies include: TCS (Ninja), Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, L&T, HCL, Tech Mahindra.

### Single-Offer & Upgrade Policy:
- A student securing a Regular offer is eligible to attempt drives for a **Dream** or **Super Dream** offer.
- Once a student secures a **Super Dream** offer, they exit the placement process to allow opportunities for remaining batchmates.

## 6. Career Development Centre (CDC) Training

The CDC starts preparing students right from their 3rd semester through mandatory non-credit and credit modules:
- **Soft Skills & Communication Training**: Public speaking, group discussions, corporate etiquette.
- **Quantitative Aptitude & Logical Reasoning**: Intensive practice sessions for written clearing rounds.
- **Coding & Technical Bootcamps**: Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), Competitive Programming, Web Development, System Design.
- **Mock Interviews**: Conducted by alumni and industry experts to eliminate interview anxiety.

---

## 7. Internship Programs & Semester Abroad

- **8th Semester Internship**: Students can dedicate their final semester to full-time industry internships (6 months to 1 year). Many Super Dream & Dream companies hire interns who transition into permanent Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).
- **International Internships**: Opportunities in USA, Germany, Japan, Singapore, and Taiwan via institutional collaborations.
