# Module 09: Research Centers, VITTBI Incubator & Intellectual Property

## 1. Overview of Research Culture

VIT places a massive emphasis on interdisciplinary research, innovation, and entrepreneurship. It is recognized as an **Institution of Eminence (IoE)** with state-of-the-art research laboratories and substantial research funding from national agencies like DST, DBAT, ISRO, DRDO, CSIR, and BIRAC.

---

## 2. VITTBI (VIT Technology Business Incubator)

**VITTBI** is a premier technology incubator established joint venture with the **Department of Science and Technology (DST)**, Government of India.

### Key Incubation Services:
- **Seed Funding Support**: Offers seed capital grants (up to ₹10 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs) for student and faculty startups under schemes like DST PRAYAS, NIDHI-EIR, and TIDE 2.0.
- **Infrastructure**: High-speed internet, dedicated co-working bays, prototyping labs, 3D printing facilities, and legal compliance consulting.
- **Mentorship**: Direct access to venture capitalists (VCs), angel investors, patent attorneys, and industry leaders.
- **Success Stories**: Has incubated over **100+ technology startups** across AI, IoT, Healthcare, CleanTech, and EdTech.

## 4. Analytical Instrumentation & Research Infrastructure

Researchers at VIT have direct access to high-end characterization tools:
- **HR-TEM** (High-Resolution Transmission Electron Microscope) & **FE-SEM** (Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope).
- **NMR Spectrometer** (Nuclear Magnetic Resonance - 400 MHz).
- **XRD** (X-Ray Diffractometer) & **XPS** (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy).
- **Confocal Laser Scanning Microscope**.
- High-Performance Computing (HPC) GPU clusters for deep learning and molecular simulations.
