# Module 06: Campus Life, Hostels, Disciplinary Rules, Fests & Clubs

## 1. Hostel Accommodation & Infrastructure

VIT is a fully residential university campus boasting state-of-the-art hostel complexes designed for safety, comfort, and academic focus.

### Men's Hostel (MH) Infrastructure (Vellore Main Campus):
- Comprises over 18 major blocks (Blocks A through Q, including high-rise modern towers).
- Features single, double, triple, 4-bed, and 6-bed rooms in both Air-Conditioned (AC) and Non-AC variants.
- Equipped with lifts, high-speed Wi-Fi, study halls, indoor game rooms (table tennis, carrom, chess), TV rooms, and solar hot water facilities.

### Women's Hostel (LH - Ladies Hostel) Infrastructure:
- Comprises modern secure blocks (Blocks A through G) surrounded by lush gardens and dedicated security checkpoints.
- Features individual study cubicles, AC/Non-AC room options, in-house beauty parlors, gyms, juice centers, and dedicated dining halls.

## 2. Hostel Rules & Disciplinary Guidelines

VIT maintains high standards of discipline to ensure student safety and well-being.

### Key Rules & Timings:
1. **Curfew & In-Time**:
   - **Men's Hostels**: Students must return to their respective hostel blocks by **9:00 PM** daily.
   - **Women's Hostels**: In-time is **8:30 PM** (weekdays) and **9:00 PM** (weekends/holidays).
2. **Biometric Attendance**:
   - Students must register their daily attendance electronically via biometric fingerprint scanners between **8:30 PM and 10:00 PM**.
3. **Outing & Leave Permissions**:
   - **Local Outing**: Short outings during non-class hours require digital gate pass authorization via the VTOP portal.
   - **Home / Night Leave**: Applied through VTOP with parent approval verification (automated SMS / IVR confirmation sent to registered parent mobile numbers).
4. **Anti-Ragging Policy (Zero Tolerance)**:
   - VIT enforces a strict **Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy** compliant with UGC and Supreme Court mandates. Any instance of ragging leads to immediate expulsion and law enforcement reporting.
5. **Substance Ban**:
   - Campus is a strict **No-Smoking, Alcohol-Free, and Drug-Free Zone**. Possession or consumption results in immediate suspension, hefty fines, and dismissal.

## 3. Major University Festivals & Events

### A. Riviera (International Cultural & Sports Festival)
- **Overview**: One of India's premier multi-genre college cultural festivals, organized annually at VIT Vellore.
- **Duration & Footfall**: Spans 4 days, drawing over **40,000+ students** from 100+ national and international institutions.
- **Key Attractions**:
  - **Pro-Shows**: Star-studded musical concerts featuring top Indian and international artists (e.g., A.R. Rahman, Sunidhi Chauhan, Amit Trivedi, Benny Dayal, Salim-Sulaiman, DJ KSHMR).
  - **Competitions**: Western & Eastern dance battles, Battle of the Bands, Fashion Show (L'Amour), theatrical plays, street plays, and sports tournaments (Cricket, Football, Basketball, Volleyball, Swimming).

### B. graVITas (International Technical Festival)
- **Overview**: VIT's flagship annual technological extravaganza focused on engineering innovation, software development, and scientific breakthroughs.
- **Key Highlights**:
  - **Hackathons**: 24 to 36-hour coding marathons sponsored by major tech corporations.
  - **RoboWars & Drone Races**: High-octane arena combat robotics and aerial drone navigation events.
  - **Guest Lectures & Workshops**: Keynotes by global scientists, tech leaders, and innovators.

## 4. Student Clubs, Chapters & Student Welfare Office (SWO)

The **Student Welfare Office (SWO)** coordinates over 100+ active student clubs and international technical chapters across campuses:

### Technical Chapters & Coding Clubs:
- **IEEE Student Branch (VIT)**: One of the largest student chapters in the Asia-Pacific region.
- **ACM & ACM-W Chapter**: Computer machinery research, competitive coding, and open-source contributions.
- **GDSC (Google Developer Student Club)**: Android, Web development, Cloud, and Machine Learning workshops.
- **CodeChef VIT & CSI (Computer Society of India)**: Weekly coding contests and algorithm bootcamps.

### Automotive & Hardware Teams (Formula Student):
- **Pravega Racing**: Combustion-engine Formula Student racing team competing in Formula SAE events globally (Germany, Italy, India).
- **Ojas / Veloce**: Electric vehicle (EV) Formula Student racing team designing custom battery packs and motors.
- **Team VAYU**: Autonomous aerial vehicle and drone design team.

### Cultural & General Interest Clubs:
- **Dance Clubs**: *FEAT* (Dance crew), *Zest*, *Ubuntu*.
- **Music Clubs**: *Music Club VIT*, *Vellore Music Society*.
- **Dramatics & Debating**: *Enactus*, *VIT Model United Nations (VITMUN)*, *Debating Society*.
- **Social Impact**: *NSS (National Service Scheme)*, *Youth Red Cross*, *Rotaract Club*.

## 5. Campus Facilities & Conveniences

- **Dining & Food Courts**: On-campus commercial food outlets including Domino's Pizza, Subway, Kathi Rolls, Juice Junction, North/South Indian thali counters, and bakery cafes.
- **Health Centre & Emergency Medical Care**:
  - 24/7 Medical Health Centre staffed with resident medical officers, nurses, and emergency observation beds.
  - 24/7 Ambulance service with tie-ups to **CMC Hospital Vellore** (Christian Medical College & Hospital) for advanced emergency treatment.
- **Sports Complex & Fitness**:
  - Olympic-size swimming pools, indoor badminton courts, wood-floored basketball courts, squash courts, synthetic running tracks, and fully equipped gymnasiums with personal trainers.
- **Banking & Postal Services**: On-campus branches of Indian Bank, SBI, and multiple ATMs.
