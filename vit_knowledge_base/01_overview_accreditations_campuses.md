# Module 01: Overview, Accreditations & Campuses

## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Identity

**Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)** is one of India's premier private deemed universities, renowned globally for high-quality engineering, technology, management, science, architecture, and law education.

- **Founded**: 1984 (originally established as *Vellore Engineering College* by Dr. G. Viswanathan under the Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act).
- **University Status**: Granted Deemed-to-be University status by the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India, in 2001.
- **Institution of Eminence (IoE)**: Recognized as an *Institution of Eminence* by the Government of India, granting it operational autonomy and international research emphasis.
- **Founder & Chancellor**: Dr. G. Viswanathan (Former Member of Parliament and Minister, Govt. of Tamil Nadu).
- **Vice Presidents**: Mr. Sankar Viswanathan, Dr. Sekar Viswanathan, Mr. G.V. Selvam.
- **Tagline / Motto**: *"A place to learn, a chance to grow"* / *"Work is Worship"*.

## 4. Detailed Campus Profiles

### A. VIT Vellore (Main Campus)
- **Address**: Katpadi - Tiruvalam Road, Vellore, Tamil Nadu - 632014.
- **Nearest Transit**: Katpadi Junction Railway Station (3 km), Chennai International Airport (MAA, ~130 km), Bengaluru Airport (BLR, ~210 km).
- **Features**:
  - Houses 16 Academic Schools and over 35,000+ full-time students.
  - Infrastructure includes over 20 hostel blocks (Men's & Women's), central library (Periyar EVR Central Library with 6 floors), indoor sports complex, outdoor stadiums, 24/7 medical center, and multiple food courts.
  - Fully residential, Wi-Fi-enabled green campus with solar power generation and sewage treatment facilities.

### B. VIT Chennai Campus
- **Address**: Vandalur - Kelambakkam Road, Chennai, Tamil Nadu - 600127.
- **Nearest Transit**: Tambaram Railway Station (15 km), Chennai Airport (MAA, ~25 km).
- **Features**:
  - Close proximity to major IT corridors (OMR, SIPCOT IT Park Siruseri, Mahindra World City).
  - Modern high-rise hostel blocks, state-of-the-art computing labs, robotics centers, and active corporate interaction modules.

### C. VIT-AP Campus (Amaravati)
- **Address**: Near Vijayawada, Inavolu, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh - 522237.
- **Nearest Transit**: Vijayawada Railway Station (25 km), Vijayawada Airport (VGA, ~45 km).
- **Features**:
  - Focuses heavily on technology and interdisciplinary engineering.
  - Offers specialized B.Tech programs in AI, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, Robotics, and Law (B.A. LL.B, B.B.A LL.B).

### D. VIT Bhopal Campus
- **Address**: Bhopal-Indore Highway, Kothri Kalan, Sehore, Madhya Pradesh - 466114.
- **Nearest Transit**: Bhopal Junction (65 km), Raja Bhoj Airport Bhopal (BHO, ~60 km).
- **Features**:
  - Pioneer in Collaborative Active Learning (CAL) methodology.
  - Emphasizes Gaming Technology, AI/ML, E-Commerce, and Cyber Security.
