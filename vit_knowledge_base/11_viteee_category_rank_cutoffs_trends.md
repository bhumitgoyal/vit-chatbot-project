# Module 11: VITEEE Category Rank Cutoffs & Counseling Strategy

## 1. Overview of Category Rank Allocation

VIT does not release official document-based cutoff ranks. However, based on historical seat allotment trends from thousands of student counseling records, clear closing rank patterns exist across campuses, branches, and fee categories (**Category 1 to Category 5**).

- **Category 1**: Lowest tuition fee, highest merit rank requirement.
- **Category 5**: Highest tuition fee, flexible merit rank requirement.

## 5. Strategic Counseling Guidance for Applicants

1. **Choice Filling Priority Order**:
   - Always fill lower-fee categories first (Category 1 $\rightarrow$ Category 2 $\rightarrow$ Category 3 $\rightarrow$ Category 4 $\rightarrow$ Category 5).
   - If a student qualifies for Category 1 in CSE Vellore, the system will allocate Category 1. You cannot skip to a higher category if a lower category seat is available for your rank.
2. **Branch vs Campus Decision**:
   - Top ranks often choose **CSE Vellore Category 1 or 2**.
   - Ranks between 15,000 and 30,000 face a choice: **CSE Chennai (Category 2/3)** or **CSE Vellore (Category 4/5)** vs **ECE Vellore (Category 2)**.
   - Because placements are **centralized via CDC**, studying CSE at Chennai or AP provides equal placement opportunities as Vellore, making lower category seats at Chennai/AP an attractive financial option.
3. **Phase Participation**:
   - Seats allotted in Phase 1 (Ranks 1 to 20,000) fill up Category 1 & 2 for CSE Vellore rapidly. Candidates MUST participate in their allotted phase date.
