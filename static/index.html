VITopia AI — Documentation

Guide to conversational features, live VTOP synchronization, and Google Cloud deployment.

1. Conversational authentication

Direct in-chat login

Sign into a VTOP account directly in the chat stream. No external dashboard or browser extension is required.

login <username> <password>

CAPTCHA: the agent reads the VTOP CAPTCHA automatically with Vertex AI vision. If auto-solve fails it renders the image in chat — reply with the code.

Logging out

Clear the active session and its stored credentials:

logout

2. Academic & campus queries

Timetable & venues

Any day's schedule, theory vs lab slots, room locations:

"What is my timetable for Wednesday?"
"Where is my Malware Analysis lab class?"

Attendance & debar

Live attendance percentages, classes needed to reach 75%, and 9.0+ pointer waiver eligibility:

"Check my live attendance and debar status for all courses."
"How many classes can I miss in Web Application Security?"

Hostel & mess

Pulls records from Services → Profile → Hostel:

"What is my hostel block and room status?"
"What mess am I enrolled in and what are the meal timings?"

Faculty directory

Deans and HoDs across all VIT schools, plus a live per-name email/cabin lookup and every teacher of your enrolled subjects:

"Who is my Dean and HoD?"
"Email and cabin for Dr. Ushus Elizebeth Zachariah"
"Who teaches my courses? Give their email and cabin."

Marks, seating & assignments

Continuous assessment scores, exam venues and seat numbers, pending digital assignments with due dates:

"Show my CAT-1 marks and upcoming exam seating."
"What are my pending digital assignments and due dates?"

3. Google Cloud architecture

Infrastructure

  • LLM inference: Vertex AI (gemini-2.5-flash) in project nuveroai (us-central1).
  • Container: Cloud Run serverless container with horizontal autoscaling.
  • Sessions: per-user VTOP session in memory, keyed by user_id; 5-minute per-module cache.
  • Surfaces: this web client and a Telegram bot share one process_chat() pipeline.
